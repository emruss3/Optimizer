# Live street detail proof

Run the app locally and visit `/qa/live-street-proof/index.html`.
The fixture places the dimensioned catalog West Heiman B2, E2 and F2 designs through normal plan placement on controlled lots. It is not surveyed or as-built. The component is the app's WebGL renderer, with no streaming, services or generated stills required for 3D.

- Select a lot, then use **Street**, **Front entry**, **Garage** or **Focus**. Drag to orbit and scroll to zoom.
- Compare captures at the same window size, focus home, preset and solar time. At an oblique angle, inspect whether each pane sits behind the street-side finish, the sill projects, and the underside follows the actual cantilever.
- Entry planting is illustrative and limited to verified clearance within a fixture lot. It must not introduce a walk, alter grading or change placement or yield.
- Product build entry points do not include this QA page. This proof is not itself a live photorealism pass or a GPU performance test.
