built homes and **not** renders derived from the Parcel Proforma plan geometry
(rooms, openings, or elevations). They must not be treated as construction
documents or as evidence that a specific plan was built. They are **not** licensed
stock photography.

Prompts asked for Middle Tennessee residential character matching each concept’s
stated setting (stories, porch/garage presence) and an orthographic facade view
per face. Existing exterior WebPs were passed as style references. The images may
still disagree with schematic room drawings in Home Studio — the Elevations tab
defaults to photoreal with honesty labeling; a secondary **Diagram** control keeps
the schematic SVG from plan volumes.

For **buildable** stock plans with publisher citations, see
`src/features/site-plan/housing/catalogue.ts` (e.g. America’s Best House Plans
1754-00010). Construction packages require a separate license from the
publisher; this repo only cites public listing facts. Do not use ABHP copyrighted
elevation drawings here.
