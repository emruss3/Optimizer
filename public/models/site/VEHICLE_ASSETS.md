# Vehicle scenery

The active assets are optimized derivatives of these original Innerscene models, both marked CC0 1.0 Universal by the publisher.

- sedan-v2.glb — [White four-door sedan](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/white-four-door-sedan-1f271353)
- suv-v2.glb — [Generic compact SUV](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/generic-compact-suv-9f157ee5)

Optimized in Blender by separating and joining surfaces by material, reducing triangle counts, removing small hidden details, and using opaque PBR materials to keep the placed fleet inexpensive. The body, swept glazing, wheel arches, mirrors, lamps and tire scale are retained. Geometry is authored in metres with its nose toward −X in glTF coordinates. These are illustration assets and are never used to count parking or prove access.

The unversioned models remain to serve already-open clients during rollout.
