Six test sites, drawn as Optimizer site plan sheets

Every sheet below is drawn by the app's own site plan renderer, on the real parcel with its real streets and neighbours. For each site, look at three sheets in order: the plan the civil got approved, what the pretrained model drew for the same site, and the closest approved plan we already had.

How to read a sheet: the dash-dot line is the property, cream shapes with a dark edge are buildings, grey is drive and parking pavement, pale shapes outside the line are neighbouring buildings and lots. Trees are the renderer's own planting, not part of any plan. Story counts read "1 story" because the layout data carries no stories. The six sites were picked by rule from the scores (the typical better case and the typical worse case per product), not by how they look. Scores are error against the civil's plan; lower is better.
